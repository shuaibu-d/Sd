## App name
SD Studio — Social Design & Video

## Short description
Create branded social posts and short videos directly on your device.

## Full description
Turn ideas into polished social content with SD Studio.

Create branded graphics and short videos using ready-made templates, your own images, brand colours, and logo. Write a script, organise it into scenes, record voice-overs, preview your video, and export it for popular social platforms.

**Design tools**
- Customisable templates for quotes, announcements, events, promotions, testimonials, and more.
- Brand kit with reusable colours and logo assets.
- Multiple canvas ratios for social posts and video formats.
- Caption tools and export options for polished content.

**Video tools**
- Script-to-scene editor with editable scene text and backgrounds.
- Voice-over recording and playback.
- Quick preview with scene timeline controls.
- H.264/AAC video export, with 1080p presets and a 720p fallback.
- Optional burned-in captions, progress indicator, and background music.

**Designed for local use**
Your projects and recordings are stored on your device. No account is required. You decide when to share or move your exported content to another app.

Some export capabilities depend on your phone's available storage and media codec support. A lower-resolution export is available when high-resolution encoding is not supported.
