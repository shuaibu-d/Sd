# SD Studio Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 2 November 2026**

SD Studio is an on-device design and video creation app. The app does not require an account and does not operate an SD Studio cloud service for storing your projects.

## Information and files used

The app uses content you create or import, including project text, images, video clips, brand assets, voice recordings, export settings, and a local diagnostic crash log. These items are stored in the app's private storage or in the device's shared Movies/SD Studio folder when you export a video.

## Where data is stored and shared

Project data, imported media references, voice recordings, settings, and diagnostic logs are kept on your device. SD Studio does not transmit this content to an SD Studio server. Android app backup is disabled. If you choose Share, use another app, or move an exported file, the receiving app or destination handles that content under its own privacy policy.

## Permissions

Microphone access is used only when recording voice-over. Media files are selected through Android's system document picker. Notification permission may be requested to show export progress. Exported videos are saved to Movies/SD Studio.

## Retention and deletion

You can delete projects and exported files using the app or Android file-management tools. Clear Cache removes temporary preview and export working files; it does not delete saved projects or completed exports. Uninstalling the app removes its private app data. Files explicitly exported to shared storage may remain until you delete them.

## Children's privacy

SD Studio is a general-purpose creative tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

## Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when app behavior changes. For privacy questions, contact the developer using the support contact published with the app listing.

This is a draft and should be checked against the final release build, Play Data safety declarations, and the developer's real support contact before publication.
